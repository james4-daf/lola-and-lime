About the vibe

Lola & Lime is a handmade, high-contrast treasure hunt — retro-kitsch, a little tiki, a lot of cut-and-paste. Think 60s–70s scrapbook pages taped over a flea-market tablecloth, not a minimal web shop.

“If it glows, clinks, or looks like it survived a backyard luau — it belongs here.”
